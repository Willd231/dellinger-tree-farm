Our Story

Three decades on the same mountain ground

Dellinger's Wholesale Nursery was founded in 1989 by Jack and Tommy Dellinger, with a simple idea: grow healthy, hardy plants and treat every customer like a neighbor.

More than thirty years later, the nursery is still run by the Dellinger family on Old River Hill Road in Spruce Pine. Our plants are grown in the same mountain soil and climate they came from, so they're ready for the real conditions of Western North Carolina.

Whether you're a landscaper filling a big order or a homeowner looking for the right tree, you'll get honest advice and a handshake you can count on.

In loving memory of Jack and Tommy Dellinger, who planted the first rows.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you sell to the public, or only to businesses?

Both. We work with landscapers and businesses, and we're glad to help local homeowners too. Give us a call or send a message and we'll help you find what you need.

How much is delivery?

Delivery is a flat $100 anywhere within 50 miles of the nursery. For locations farther out, call us at (828) 765-9593 and we'll talk it through.

Is there a minimum order?

Please contact us about order minimums. We're happy to go over the details for your project.

How do I know what's in stock?

Check the inventory list on this page, or call us for the most up-to-date availability. Stock changes through the season.

Can I come by and see the plants?

Yes. We're at 31 Old River Hill Rd in Spruce Pine. Please call ahead so someone is ready to walk the rows with you.
